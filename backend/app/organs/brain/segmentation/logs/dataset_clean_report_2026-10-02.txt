4. SPLIT
================================================================================
Grouped on the LESION, not the file. Group key = filename stem with the
sequence prefix (T1 - / T1C+ - / T2 -) and the trailing slice number removed.

  train    8,044 slices  (80.0%)    281 lesions
  val      1,006 slices  (10.0%)     33 lesions
  test     1,006 slices  (10.0%)     33 lesions

Balanced by slice count. Seeded and deterministic.

WHY GROUPING MATTERS
  The raw archive contains 300 lesions that appear under two or three sequence
  prefixes, covering 842 series. For example "Meningioma frontal" exists as
  "T1 - Meningioma frontal", "T1C+ - Meningioma frontal" and
  "T2 - Meningioma frontal" - the same lesion, three separate series.
  Splitting by file would place that lesion in train and validation at the same
  time and inflate every reported metric.

  manifest.csv carries the "group" column. Use it if the split is ever redone.

5. WHAT WAS REMOVED  (1,062 files, all listed in dropped.csv)
================================================================================
  Count   Reason
  -----   ------------------------------------------------------------------
    859   duplicate image bytes within ONE lesion
          (kept the best-aligned member: highest inside-anatomy fraction,
           then larger foreground, then alphabetical)

    173   duplicate image bytes claiming TWO DIFFERENT lesions
          (the whole group was dropped - the image cannot be both and nothing
           in the files identifies which label is correct, so it is unusable
           rather than merely duplicated)

     30   mask sits below 90% on anatomy
          (one at 0.000 - entirely off the head)

  Reconciliation:  10,056 kept + 1,062 dropped = 11,118

THE PROBLEM THIS FIXED
  In the raw archive, 1,768 files share image bytes while carrying a DIFFERENT
  mask (810 distinct image hashes). In some cases the two masks do not overlap
  at all (IoU = 0.000). That is identical model input with contradictory
  supervision - not noise a model can average away. The strict build resolves
  every such case to a single label.

6. CHECKED AND CLEARED  (not removed)
================================================================================
  Cross-lesion perceptual near-duplicates
    55 pairs at Hamming distance <= 3 on a 64-bit DCT perceptual hash.
    Direct pixel comparison: the closest pair differs by a mean absolute error
    of 5.3 grey levels, most at 15-30. ZERO pairs are the same image.
    These are genuinely similar-looking slices of the same tumour type, not
    duplicates. They stay in.

  pHash threshold calibration
    The usual default of 6 is too loose for medical images - it matches on
    shared composition (brain centred, dark surround) rather than content.
    Sweeping the threshold: the same-group / cross-group ratio is flat at
    ~0.044 through threshold 3, then rises to 0.088 at 4 and 0.227 at 6.
    Use 3 on this dataset, not 6.

  Blank / low-contrast slices
    None. Minimum grayscale standard deviation is 17.2.
