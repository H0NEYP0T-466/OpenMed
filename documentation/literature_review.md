# Master Systematic Literature Review: Multi-Organ Medical AI, Assistive Systems, and Deployment Architecture

> **Executive Core Thesis:** Across more than a decade of medical artificial intelligence research spanning hundreds of models and benchmark challenges, an insurmountable translational chasm persists. Over 95% of published works are strictly isolated, single-organ, single-task algorithms evaluated on curated retrospective benchmarks and implemented as standalone Python scripts or offline Jupyter notebooks. 
> 
> Multi-organ generalists such as TotalSegmentator segment normal anatomical boundaries on CT scans, but completely fail to identify, classify, or segment multi-organ diseases and tumors. Meanwhile, state-of-the-art vision-language report generators and clinical LLMs operate on 2D chest radiographs or isolated textual prompts, remaining completely disconnected from dense spatial 3D segmentation masks. 
>
> **OpenMed** is the first medical diagnostic platform engineered to bridge this entire pipeline: unifying 10+ organ pathology classification and lesion segmentation with automated structured clinical reporting, interactive 3D whole-body and organ anatomical visualization, a conversational clinical decision support assistant, and an integrated, production-grade FastAPI backend and React frontend.
